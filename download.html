Three places to get it, depending on what you are holding.

Android

The full app: alignment search, reminders, and home-screen widgets. You can also share a map link straight into it to set a location.

Get it on Google Play

iPhone & iPad

Adds Lock Screen widgets and a share extension, so a map link shared from another app can become your position or your target. No ads.

Download on the App Store

Chrome

A popup version for the desktop: set two points, see the countdown, and get a browser notification when the alignment is close. No ads.
