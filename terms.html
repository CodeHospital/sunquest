Description of Service

SunQuest calculates the moment the sun's position aligns with the bearing between a starting location and a target location — any destination, landmark, or other location you choose — and reminds you beforehand with notifications and home-screen widgets (browser notifications in the Chrome extension). The app is provided as a convenience and informational tool for sun-powered, natural navigation.
