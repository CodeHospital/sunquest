Know Exactly When To Face The Sun

SunQuest calculates the precise moment each day when the sun aligns with any target location you choose — a destination, a landmark, a loved one's city — then reminds you with notifications and a live home-screen countdown. No compass, no magnetometer, no continuous GPS tracking.

Key Features

🧭

Sun-Alignment Calculator

Finds the exact date and time the sun's position matches the bearing to your target — an ancient shadow-based navigation method, done automatically.

🔔

Smart Reminders

Get notified 60, 30, 10, 5, and 1 minute before alignment, plus a final alert right when it happens.

📲

Home Screen Widgets

Keep a live countdown to the next alignment right on your home screen — no need to open the app.

🌍

Any Target, Anywhere

Set any target location on Earth — a destination, a landmark, or someone you care about — and know exactly when the sun points the way.
